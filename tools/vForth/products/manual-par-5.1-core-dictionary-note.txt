Introductory note for section 5.1 "The 'core' dictionary" -- to be pasted by
hand right under the heading, before the first alphabetical entry ('0x00').
Clarifies the core / non-core ambiguity raised by adding THRU (2026-07-02)
to this alphabetical list, and by the fact that many non-core words are
NOT here at all -- they live in their own dedicated chapter instead.
Drafted 2026-07-02.

======================================================================

Despite its title, this alphabetical list documents two kinds of
definitions. Core definitions are compiled into forth18e.bin and are
always present at startup; they need no loading at all. Extension
definitions live as source files in the inc/ and lib/ directories of the
SD card and enter the dictionary only on request, via NEEDS: their entry
ends with the sentence "This definition is available after NEEDS cccc".
Every entry without that closing sentence describes a core definition.
The interactive HELP (sec. 2.2.1) follows the same convention, with the
reminder "Available after NEEDS cccc" on the last line of its pages.

This list is not, however, the only place extension words are
documented, and most of them are not here at all. Whenever a library
module is large or coherent enough to deserve its own narrative --
Graphics mode and Layer facility, Mouse, Floating-Point Option, the
ASSEMBLER vocabulary, AY sound chip facility, Keyboard input, the Heap
Memory Facility -- it gets a dedicated section of its own earlier (or,
for Heap and Testing, as a titled subsection later) in this manual, with
its own worked examples. Such a word is documented once, in that
section, and is not repeated here. This alphabetical list is reserved
for the core vocabulary plus the smaller extension words that do not
warrant a section of their own (TO, VALUE, WITHIN, THROW, ENUMERATED,
THRU, ...). When looking for a word and not finding it here, check the
Table of Contents for a section named after the feature it belongs to
before concluding it does not exist.
