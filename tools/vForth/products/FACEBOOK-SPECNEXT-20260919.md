## Standard version

To everyone whose Issue 3 machine is about to arrive: I am one of you, a backer of this campaign and still waiting for news, so I share the excitement! 🎉

Some of you may know vForth, a free and open source (MIT) Forth for NextZXOS that I have been developing for a long time. It has never stopped evolving, with one aim: to give Forth access to what the Next offers, from Layer 2 and sprites to sound, DMA and the Copper. If you are curious about programming your Next, Forth may be a fun way to do it. It comes with a guided tutorial made of more than 60 lessons, from "1 2 + ." to those hardware features, and about 450 help pages, one per word, available on the machine with HELP. Everything is at https://github.com/mattsteeldue/vforth-next

In the same spirit of evolution, you can now also explore the environment from Visual Studio Code, with an extension in the util/ folder of the same repository: colouring, checks on what to load with NEEDS, and a command that copies the file you are editing into the CSpect SD image while the emulator is running. It is a first release, and entirely optional: vForth works the same without it.

Questions and suggestions are welcome, here or on GitHub. Happy hacking! 🐉
